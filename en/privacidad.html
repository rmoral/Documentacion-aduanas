Privacy policy

Last updated · August 2026

1. Data controller

AduanaFácil Andorra, a document preparation service based in the Principality of Andorra, is the controller of the personal data provided through this website.

2. Data we process

  • Sender's data: full name, identity document, phone, address in Andorra and email.
  • Recipient's data: full name, identity document, phone and delivery address in Spain.
  • Shipment data: description of the goods, declared value and planned date.
  • Payment data: payment is processed through Stripe; we do not store card details.

3. Purpose and legal basis

We process the data exclusively to prepare the requested customs documentation, manage the payment for the service and handle subsequent revisions or queries. The legal basis is the performance of the contract for the services requested by the client.

4. Recipients

The data is not shared with third parties, except for the providers needed to deliver the service (payment processor, email provider and, where applicable, the form-handling service) and any applicable legal obligations. The prepared documents are delivered only to the client.

5. Retention

The data is kept for as long as the service is being provided and, afterwards, for the periods needed to meet legal responsibilities.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to AduanaFácil Andorra at the contact address included in the communications of your order. You can also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Andorra, the Agència Andorrana de Protecció de Dades).

7. Cookies and local storage

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It only uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) to temporarily keep your order reference and show you the confirmation; that information never leaves your device.
